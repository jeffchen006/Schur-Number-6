Layout and visual references
============================

The paper uses the standard LaTeX article document class, with a single column,
a three-column author block showing affiliations and emails on the first page,
a compact abstract, theorem environments, and numbered figure and table captions.

Published papers read for the presentation:

1. H. Fredricksen and M. M. Sweet, Symmetric Sum-Free Partitions and Lower
   Bounds for Schur Numbers, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 7 (2000), R32.
   https://doi.org/10.37236/1510
   The constructions on page 6 list explicit color classes. Our paper also
   retains a complete numerical certificate near the main result.

2. M. J. H. Heule, Schur Number Five, Proceedings of AAAI 32(1) (2018).
   https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v32i1.12209
   Figure 1 places integer positions in columns and color classes in rows.
   Our new diagram uses this row layout with our own 537 certificate.

Additional table reference:

3. R. Ageron et al., New lower bounds for Schur and weak Schur numbers,
   arXiv:2112.03175v2 (2022), Table 1.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.03175v2
   This preprint compares earlier bounds and the authors' improvements.
   Our Table 1 uses a similar old/new comparison with the values derived here.

All new figure cells and family statistics are generated from certificate.col
and solutions537.txt by make_figures.py. No source-paper image is embedded
or copied. The family table describes the retained sample. Implementation details and the historical decoder caveat are documented in
the repository, outside the paper. The paper presents only the mathematical
construction, explicit partitions, figures, tables, and references.
