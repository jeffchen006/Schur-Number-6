Layout and presentation references
============================

The paper uses the standard LaTeX article document class, with a single column,
an arXiv reference below the title,
a compact abstract, theorem environments, and numbered table captions.

Published papers read for the presentation:

1. H. Fredricksen and M. M. Sweet, Symmetric Sum-Free Partitions and Lower
   Bounds for Schur Numbers, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 7 (2000), R32.
   https://doi.org/10.37236/1510
   The constructions on page 6 list explicit color classes. Our paper also
   retains a complete numerical certificate near the main result.

Additional table reference:

2. R. Ageron et al., New lower bounds for Schur and weak Schur numbers,
   arXiv:2112.03175v2 (2022), Table 1.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.03175v2
   This preprint compares earlier bounds and the authors' improvements.
   Our Table 1 uses a similar old/new comparison with the values derived here.

The paper contains no figures. It retains the explicit partition, mathematical
frame conditions, bound comparison table, and family statistics table.
Implementation details and the historical decoder caveat are documented in
the repository, outside the paper.
